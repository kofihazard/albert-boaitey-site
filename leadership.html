Leadership & Engagement

Africa Mind Mine Initiative (AMMI)

I co-lead the Africa Mind Mine Initiative (AMMI), a pan‑African platform dedicated to cultivating the continent’s intellectual capital and strengthening its global voice. AMMI brings together scholars, professionals, policymakers, and youth to advance foresight, collective intelligence, and evidence‑based development.

Website: africamind.org

Areas of Leadership

Knowledge & Foresight: Research-driven insights on Africa’s food systems, sustainability, and development pathways.

Collective Intelligence: Connecting African talent across disciplines and regions.

Public Engagement: Translating research into accessible narratives for policymakers, media, and the public.

Mentorship: Supporting emerging scholars and practitioners across Africa and the diaspora.
