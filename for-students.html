Supervision Philosophy

I aim to create a supportive, intellectually rigorous environment where students can develop strong analytical skills, independent thinking, and a clear academic identity.

What I Expect

  • Ownership of your project
  • Prepared, structured meetings
  • Openness to revision and iteration
  • Professional communication

What You Can Expect

  • Clear, constructive feedback
  • Support with framing, methods, and publication strategy
  • Mentorship on conferences and career development
