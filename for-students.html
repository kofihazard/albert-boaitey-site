For Students

Supervision Philosophy

I aim to create a supportive, intellectually rigorous environment where students can develop strong analytical skills, independent thinking, and a clear academic identity. My supervision style is collaborative, structured, and responsive.

What I Expect From Students

Ownership: You drive your project; I help you sharpen it.

Communication: Regular updates and prepared meetings.

Revisions: Openness to feedback and iterative improvement.

Professionalism: Respect for deadlines, colleagues, and collaborators.

What You Can Expect From Me

Feedback: Clear, constructive, and timely.

Support: Help with framing, methods, and publication strategy.

Mentorship: Guidance on conferences, networks, and careers.
