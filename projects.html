Research Projects

Corporate Food Net Zero

This project examines how food companies design and implement net‑zero strategies, and how these strategies affect farmers, supply chains, and sustainability outcomes.

Website: corporatefoodnetzero.com

Calf Management Practices & Social Sustainability

Funded by USDA NIFA and the Wisconsin Dairy Innovation Hub, this project investigates farmer practices, welfare outcomes, and public attitudes toward calf‑dam separation.

Genome Canada Dairy Resilience

A multi‑partner initiative integrating genomic approaches to improve dairy cattle resilience and sustainability in Canada.

Net‑Zero Incentives & Behavioural Responses

Funded by AFN Network+, this project maps incentives for change in the transition to net zero across agricultural systems.

Women in Shea Butter Value Chains

A scoping study funded by ISPF‑ODA examining gender, livelihoods, and value chain dynamics in shea butter production.
