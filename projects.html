Current Projects

My research spans livestock sustainability, animal welfare, food systems policy, and the behavioral economics of agricultural decision-making. Below is a selection of ongoing and recently completed projects supported by international funders.

Major Research Projects

Calf Management Practices & Social Sustainability

USDA NIFA • PI

Examining producer decision-making, welfare outcomes, and public attitudes toward calf management practices in the dairy sector.

Dairy Cattle Resilience & Genomic Innovation

Genome Canada • Co‑Applicant

Integrating genomic tools to improve resilience and sustainability in dairy production systems across Canada.

Net‑Zero Incentives in Agricultural Systems

AFN Network+ • Co‑Applicant

Mapping behavioral, economic, and institutional incentives for climate mitigation in livestock and dairy systems.

Women in Shea Value Chains

ISPF‑ODA • PI

A scoping study on gender, livelihoods, and value chain participation in West African shea production.

Research Themes

  • Livestock genomics and sustainability
  • Animal welfare and public attitudes
  • Food systems policy and technology acceptance
  • Environmental incentives and carbon mitigation
  • Behavioral economics of producer decision-making
