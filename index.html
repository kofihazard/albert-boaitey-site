Albert K. Boaitey

PhD · Agricultural & Resource Economics

I am a Senior Lecturer at the School of Agriculture and Environment, Massey University New Zealand. I received a PhD in Agricultural and Resource Economics from the University of Alberta, Canada. I completed my Bachelor and Master of Science degrees in Agricultural Economics at the University of Ghana and the University of Saskatchewan, Canada, respectively. I am an Associated Graduate Faculty member at the University of Guelph, Canada, and a Visiting Lecturer at Newcastle University.Previously, I served on the faculty at the University of Wisconsin–River Falls, USA, and Newcastle University, UK. I am the Lead Researcher for the Corporate Food Net Zero Initiative and a co‑founder of Africa Mind Mine.

Research Focus

My work examines livestock sustainability, climate mitigation, animal welfare, food systems, and agricultural policy. I use empirical, behavioural, and stated‑preference methods to understand how producers, consumers, and institutions respond to environmental and technological change.
