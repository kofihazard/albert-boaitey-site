Albert K. Boaitey

Senior Lecturer · Agricultural & Resource Economics

I hold a PhD in Agricultural and Resource Economics from the University of Alberta, Canada. I completed my Bachelor and Master of Science degrees in Agricultural Economics at the University of Ghana and the University of Saskatchewan, Canada, respectively. Previously, I served on the faculty at the University of Wisconsin–River Falls, USA, and Newcastle University, UK. Currently, I am an Associated Graduate Faculty member at the University of Guelph, Canada, and a Visiting Lecturer at Newcastle University. I also serve as the Lead Researcher for the Corporate Food Net Zero Initiative and am a co‑founder of Africa Mind Mine.

Research Focus

My work examines livestock sustainability, climate mitigation, animal welfare, food systems, and agricultural policy. I use empirical, behavioural, and stated‑preference methods to understand how producers, consumers, and institutions respond to environmental and technological change.
