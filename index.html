About

I am an agricultural and resource economist working at the intersection of livestock systems, sustainability, animal welfare, and food systems policy. My research examines how producers, consumers, and institutions respond to environmental and ethical challenges in agriculture, with a focus on livestock genomics, greenhouse gas mitigation, and societal attitudes toward meat and animal welfare.

Research

Sustainable Livestock Systems
Genomic selection, feed efficiency, environmental impacts, carbon offset design.

Farm Animal Welfare & Societal Attitudes
Public and farmer perceptions, ethical food choice, social desirability bias.

Agricultural Policy & Markets
Food technology concerns, sustainability incentives, dairy and plant-based markets.

Leadership

I co-lead the Africa Mind Mine Initiative (AMMI), a pan‑African platform advancing foresight, collective intelligence, and research-driven development.
