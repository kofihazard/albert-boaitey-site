About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Agricultural and Resource Economics at Massey University, New Zealand. My work examines how economic incentives, policy design, and technological innovation shape sustainability outcomes in livestock systems and food supply chains.

My research integrates empirical methods, behavioural insights, and interdisciplinary collaboration to address challenges at the intersection of agriculture, environment, and society.

Research Areas

  • Livestock sustainability and climate mitigation.
  • Animal welfare, consumer behaviour, and food choice.
  • Genomic innovation and breeding for resilience.
  • Environmental policy and agricultural transitions.
  • Farmer decision‑making and institutional trust.
