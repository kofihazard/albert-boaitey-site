Research Philosophy

My work integrates economic analysis with animal science, behavioral insights, and sustainability frameworks to understand how producers, consumers, and institutions respond to environmental and ethical challenges in agriculture. I aim to generate evidence that informs policy, improves welfare outcomes, and strengthens the resilience of livestock systems.
