Research Overview

My research sits at the intersection of livestock systems, sustainability, animal welfare, consumer behaviour, and food systems policy. I am particularly interested in how economic incentives, social norms, and technological change shape the future of livestock production.

A central theme in my work is understanding how livestock systems can become environmentally sustainable, socially legitimate, and economically viable across diverse global contexts.

Research Themes

🐄 Sustainable Livestock Systems

Genomic selection, feed efficiency, environmental impacts, carbon mitigation.

Includes work on carbon offset design and beef supply chains.

🐾 Animal Welfare & Society

Public attitudes, ethical food choice, social desirability bias, dairy welfare.

Focus on calf separation, welfare labels, and public good framing.

🌍 Global Food Systems & Policy

Food technology concerns, alternative proteins, sustainability incentives.

Work on consumer preferences, credence attributes, and market transitions.

Selected Research Questions

  • How can breeding and genomic tools support climate mitigation in livestock systems?
  • What do citizens consider acceptable animal welfare practices in dairy and beef production?
  • How do climate anxiety and ethical concerns shape food choices and meat consumption?
  • What incentives and policies can support net‑zero transitions in agriculture?
  • How do global supply chains and local practices interact in shaping sustainable food systems?
