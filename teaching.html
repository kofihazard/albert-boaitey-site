Teaching

I teach across agricultural economics, food systems, and sustainability, with a focus on connecting economic theory to real-world policy and industry decisions. My teaching integrates empirical evidence from my research on livestock systems, food choice, and environmental policy.

My approach emphasizes critical thinking, interdisciplinary perspectives, and the application of economic tools to contemporary challenges in agriculture and food systems.

Courses Taught

  • Agricultural Economics — production, markets, and policy.
  • Food Systems Policy — governance, sustainability, and global transitions.
  • Research Methods — quantitative and qualitative approaches.
  • Agribusiness Management & Marketing — strategy and decision-making.
  • Natural Resource Economics — environmental valuation and policy.
  • Leadership & Interdisciplinary Practice — collaboration across domains.

Supervision

  • Primary and co-supervision of PhD students in agricultural and resource economics.
  • MSc thesis supervision on livestock systems, welfare, and sustainability.
  • Undergraduate research projects in agricultural economics and food policy.

Teaching Philosophy

I aim to create a learning environment that is analytical, inclusive, and grounded in real-world challenges. Students are encouraged to explore the economic, ethical, and environmental dimensions of agricultural systems, and to develop the skills needed to contribute to sustainable food futures.
